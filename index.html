Support

A little help.

Questions, a word suggestion, or something not working? Get in touch.

Email ShuFee Words support

shufee.app@gmail.com · Include your app version, device model, and a brief description of the issue. Please avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information.

Where should I start?

On Today, tap Start today’s words. ShuFee Words picks the next set for you, beginning with everyday greetings. Prefer a different topic? Open Topics, choose a set, and tap Make this my next set. For a quick guide, open Settings (the gear on Today) and choose How ShuFee Words works or Reading Arabizi.

How does a session work?

New words come in four short steps. Meet each word, match the set to its meanings, pick each word from four choices, then type it. Words you miss come back once at the end of the session. Reviews start with picking and move on to typing as a word gets stronger.

When do words come back for review?

A new word comes back the next day, then after longer and longer gaps: a few days, a week, two weeks, a month, and beyond. Get one wrong and it starts again from tomorrow. A word counts as known once you’ve typed it correctly on three different days. Reviews always come before new words on Today.

Can I learn more than eight words a day?

Yes. After today’s cup, tap Learn 8 more on Today. To change the daily number, open Settings and set New words a day anywhere from 5 to 20.

Why was my spelling accepted, or shown differently?

Arabizi has no official spelling. ShuFee Words accepts the same common variations as ShuFee, such as kh or 5, ch or sh, e or i, o or u, and doubled letters, plus one short vowel more or less inside a longer word and an optional final 2. Case, spacing, punctuation, and vowel marks never matter. When it accepts a variation, it shows the spelling it uses. If you type a different word from the set, it tells you which word you wrote.

Can I answer in Arabic script?

Yes. In Settings, set Answer in to Arabic script. Arabic answers are accepted with or without vowel marks. To type Arabic, add the Arabic keyboard in your device’s Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards. The Vowel marks switch shows or hides harakat wherever Arabic appears; it’s off by default.

How does the dictionary work?

Open Dictionary and search in English, Arabizi, or Arabic. Spelling can be loose. Filter by Words, Phrases, or Saved, and tap the bookmark on any word’s page to save it. Each page shows the word’s forms, a pronunciation guide with the stressed syllable marked, examples, and other words from the same set.

What does Conjugate in ShuFee do?

For the 29 verbs that ShuFee also teaches, a word’s page offers Conjugate in ShuFee, where you can practice all eight people and four tenses. ShuFee is a separate app. If you don’t have it yet, the button opens ShuFee on the App Store.

Can I hear the words?

Pronunciation recordings are on their way. When they arrive in an update, a listen button will appear beside words and examples, and Settings will let you choose a voice. Until then, each word’s Say it line shows its pronunciation, with an accent on the stressed syllable.

How do I turn off sounds or haptics?

Use the Sounds and Haptics switches in Settings. Sound effects respect your device’s Silent mode.

Where is my progress?

Your words, reviews, saved words, and preferences are stored on your device. ShuFee Words has no account or cloud-sync service, so removing the app may remove this progress. To start over, use Reset progress at the bottom of Settings.

Privacy

Your words are yours.

ShuFee Words privacy policy
Effective October 3, 2026 · Applies to ShuFee Words 1.0 for iPhone and iPad.

Information in the app

ShuFee Words does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your answers, review schedule, saved words, progress, or preferences to the developer. These records are saved locally using Apple’s device storage. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics services, and it does not track you.

Audio and your voice

Any pronunciation audio is bundled with the app; playing it sends nothing anywhere. ShuFee Words does not record your voice or request microphone access.

Links to ShuFee

Conjugate in ShuFee opens the ShuFee app or its App Store page. No information about your learning is passed to ShuFee or the App Store.

Your choices

You can change sound, haptic, and script preferences in the app, and reset your progress at any time. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to any backups managed by your device or Apple. Device backups and App Store purchases are governed by Apple’s services and privacy policies.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve the issue. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting shufee.app@gmail.com, subject to any records we must retain.

This website

This support site uses no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies, and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. The Match game runs in your browser; your answers are not saved or sent to us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an external link or sending email is subject to the relevant service provider’s practices.

Children and changes

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child sends personal information in a support message, contact us to request its deletion. Any changes to this policy will appear here with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email shufee.app@gmail.com.
